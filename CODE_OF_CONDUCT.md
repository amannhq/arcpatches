# Code of Conduct

ArcPatches follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing someone's private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept correction gracefully.

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately. Use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and mark the report as a conduct issue, or message a maintainer directly on GitHub.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
